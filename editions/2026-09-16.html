02 / COMPANIES

Multi-model stacks become the default

Google · AnthropicSeptember 15, 2026Original report: Business Insider

Google lets all of its engineers use Claude Opus 5 for internal coding

Google expanded company-wide access to Anthropic’s Claude Opus 5 through its own Antigravity agentic development platform, with per-user quotas. Access had previously been limited to select DeepMind teams and high-priority projects, while external coding tools such as Claude Code and OpenAI Codex were generally barred.

A Google spokesperson said: “Gemini remains our primary and foundational model for internal development, with third-party models available on a quota to support specialized use cases.”

Why it matters

A frontier lab giving its engineers broad access to a rival’s coding model is a striking acknowledgment of Anthropic’s strength — and of the shift from one-model loyalty to purpose-built model portfolios.

Reporting note Business Insider’s employee-source reporting was carried by the linked secondary outlets.

04 / SAFETY & POLICY

Washington and Brussels move farther apart

United StatesSeptember 14, 2026Single-sourced

Trump dismisses AI safety alarms as a “HOAX”

CNN reported that President Donald Trump spent Monday rejecting calls from AI industry leaders for more government regulation, calling the concern a “SICK conspiracy” and a “HOAX.” He compared it to what he called the “Global Warming Scam.”

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang put a call with Trump on speakerphone at a conference, where the president said “the robots will not be taking over.” Trump later told reporters he mainly uses AI to generate images for social-media posts.

Reporting note CNN is the sole source in this edition for the call details and staff-level dynamics.

European UnionSeptember 16, 2026 · Late-breaking

Von der Leyen will invite frontier labs to talks on AI risks

European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen told the European Parliament in Strasbourg that she would invite the main frontier labs to discuss how Europe could support industry efforts to “pace the frontier.”

Her speech cited concerns from former Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon, who resigned while accusing Anthropic and OpenAI of racing toward self-improving superintelligence without acting responsibly.

Why it matters

The EU is positioning the AI Act’s enforcement phase as the base for a global role in frontier-risk governance, directly counter to Washington’s hands-off line.
