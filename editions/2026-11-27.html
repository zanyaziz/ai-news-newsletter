01 / RESEARCH & BREAKTHROUGHS

Three labs, three proofs, zero verifications

#1 · Research · Anthropic / ClaudeNov 25–26, 2026Unverified — no independent check

Anthropic posts Claude-made proof that VP ≠ VNP over every field

An internal Claude research model produced a paper and Lean 4 formalization claiming VP is not equal to VNP over every field, based on lower bounds for arithmetic circuits. Anthropic says no human mathematician has checked the proof yet, and it does not resolve P versus NP. OpenAI independently produced a proof on the same problem.

Why it matters: if it holds up, it is a landmark for AI-led mathematical research — a frontier model originating a major complexity result, not just checking one.

Key people/companies: Anthropic; Claude; OpenAI (independent proof).

#2 · Research · OpenAIPreprint Nov 24, 2026Unverified preprint

OpenAI pins the matrix multiplication exponent at exactly 9/4

A preprint claims the matrix multiplication exponent ω is exactly 9/4. A companion preprint disproves the polynomial Hirsch conjecture, and OpenAI added nine math manuscripts on Nov 24 to its repository from an unreleased internal model (722 total), covering tensor geometry, strongly polynomial time linear programming, arithmetic complexity, combinatorics and polytope diameter.

Why it matters: the exponent ω governs how fast matrix multiplication — the core operation behind AI training — can in principle run, so a closed value would be a foundational result.

Key people/companies: OpenAI.

#3 · Research · Google ResearchWed, Nov 25, 2026Unverified agent-written proofs

Google Research’s Cogentic agent posts six maths papers from one prompt each

Google Research’s Cogentic agent produced six mathematics papers — one prompt each — including a proven LN[1/3, 105] factoring algorithm and sub-quasi-polynomial discrete logs.

Why it matters: long-horizon agentic research — one prompt per paper — is the harness pattern agent builders care about, and the factoring result drew wide attention even though it does not threaten RSA in practice.

#4 · Research · Google / GeminiFri, Nov 27, 2026 · Late-breaking

Gemini 4 Carbon assists proof of the 70-year-old Hadwiger conjecture

The three-dimensional covering conjecture — any d-dimensional convex body can be covered by at most 2d smaller homothetic copies — was tackled with a “2 + 6 = 8 twilight-line illumination” approach. The model generated the proof intuition and 13,700 lines of formal verification code, with two human researchers guiding it mid-process.

Why it matters: a concrete case of a frontier model contributing to original mathematical discovery rather than just problem-solving.

Key people/companies: Google; Gemini 4 Carbon.

02 / MODELS & OPEN WEIGHTS

Five open-weight releases in two days

#5 · Open Weights · NvidiaFri, Nov 27, 2026Non-commercial license

Nvidia open-sources Nemotron-Labs-Image-30B-A3B image generator

A 30B-parameter autoregressive image model (3B active per token) built on Nemotron 3 Nano, generating visual tokens sequentially rather than via diffusion. An agent-linked system scored 92.86 on GenEval versus GPT Image 2’s 89, and 97.98 on LongTextBench. Weights are on Hugging Face for non-commercial research and evaluation only.

Why it matters: Nvidia extends its open-model strategy from language into visual generation.

Key people/companies: Nvidia; Nemotron.

AISeng Tech ↗
#6 · Open Weights · Ant GroupFri, Nov 27, 2026Hallucination caution

Ant Group releases Ling-3.2-air multimodal MoE under MIT license

A 124B-parameter hybrid-linear MoE activating 5.5B parameters per token, accepting text, images and video, with a 262,144-token context window. Agentic office-task completion on AutomationBench-AA rose from 15.7% to 61% versus the prior generation.

Why it matters: the MIT license makes this a rare fully permissive multimodal open-weight option. Caveat: on Harvey LAB-AA v1.1 it reports an 87.7% criteria pass rate but only 0.8% full-pass after hallucination checks — caution for high-stakes deployment.

Key people/companies: Ant Group.
