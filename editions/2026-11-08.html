02 / RESEARCH

AI speeding up AI research, cracking legacy crypto, and entering the wet lab

#4 · AI R&D · METR / AnthropicNov 7 · METR report Nov 5Estimate uncertain — 1.3×–3× range · Some evidence withheld

METR finds Anthropic's AI is accelerating Anthropic's own research

An independent assessment by METR, an AI safety research organisation, found evidence that Anthropic's AI models are helping accelerate development of more advanced AI systems inside Anthropic — AI-assisted research contributing to faster model development, as reported by The Verge. METR emphasised substantial uncertainty: its estimate could be closer to 1.3× or 3× the historical rate, and some underlying evidence was withheld, so the extent of the effect remains subject to independent scrutiny. If the effect is real, it is an early, measured sign of AI systems speeding up the research that produces their successors.

Key players: METR, Anthropic, The Verge.

#5 · Cryptography · Google DeepMindNov 6Company-reported

DeepMind AI agents expose weaknesses in legacy 1024-bit RSA

Human-guided AI agents at Google DeepMind uncovered two major weaknesses in legacy 1024-bit RSA, announced by Pushmeet Kohli, VP of Science — with about a 1600× theoretical improvement under short fingerprint control and roughly 30× lower marginal cost for batch factoring of additional 1024-bit keys. DeepMind stressed that modern 2048-bit and larger keys remain secure. The finding matters because it shows AI agents accelerating cryptanalysis of legacy systems, sharpening the case for migrating off 1024-bit RSA.

Key players: Google DeepMind, Pushmeet Kohli.

#6 · Interpretability · MIT CSAILNov 2Blog post with logs — not peer-reviewed

Can an LLM read and rewrite neural network weights?

Rikiya Takehi, Jayden Teoh, Alex Zhang and Omar Khattab published “Sparks of Weight-Space Programming”, reporting that OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra can identify what a small network was trained to do from its raw numbers and edit weights without gradient descent — changing 0.016% of Qwen3.5-0.8B's weights to fix a reasoning loop, lifting its MATH-500 score from 7.9% to 48.1%. Critics note the headline result mixes two very different setups, so the claim needs careful reading.

Key players: MIT CSAIL, OpenAI.

06 / POLICY & SAFETY

A 2027 regulatory roadmap meets a global data-center backlash

#17 · Regulation · U.S. CongressNov 6 · ReutersLegislative roadmap — not enacted law

Democrats unveil 2027 AI-regulation roadmap: mandatory independent testing

After the new Congress convenes in January 2027, Democrats plan a legislative push centred on three issues: requiring independent experts to safety-test top AI models, clarifying AI companies' joint legal liability for product harms, and preventing data-center expansion from driving up residential electricity rates. Rep. Lori Trahan drives the testing provision (previously in the FRONTIER Act, introduced July 2026); Sen. Chuck Schumer, expected to become Senate Majority Leader, named energy costs a priority; Rep. Greg Casar advocates requiring AI executives to testify under oath. Passage is highly uncertain: President Trump has repeatedly opposed mandatory AI regulation and on Nov 7 reiterated “100 percent support for superintelligence”, and tech stocks fell sharply Nov 5 on regulatory worries.

Key players: Lori Trahan, Chuck Schumer, Greg Casar, Donald Trump.
