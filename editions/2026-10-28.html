01 / MODELS & RELEASES

A world model that makes sound, a mid-tier Terra, and six small open models

#3 · Models · Google DeepMindAnnounced Oct 27 · Research previewCompany demos only — not independently benchmarked

Google DeepMind previews Genie 4 world model with native audio

Genie 4 is DeepMind’s first real-time world model to generate audio natively in the same generative loop — music, ambient sound and interactive dialogue — rather than bolting sound on afterwards. Gemini 3.8 Flash sits in the loop as a guiding agent, and users can steer by text in real time: change the weather, move the camera, add people or objects mid-simulation. Coherent scenes now run to about 2 minutes, double Genie 3’s ~1 minute.

Access is a research preview for trusted testers only. Next comes rollout to Google AI Ultra subscribers via Project Genie and early-access Gemini API / AI Studio partners; a waitlist is open, with no firm timeline.

Caveat: company demos only, not independently benchmarked — and DeepMind itself notes that visual detail and object permanence still drift.

Anthropic files S-1 and lands $63B chip financing

Anthropic’s S-1 shows H1 2026 revenue of $16.3 billion, up ~1,300% year on year — Q2 alone brought $11.56B and the company’s first quarterly operating profit, $974M. Compute spend in H1 was about $8.9B. Read the loss correctly: the $80.7B net loss is driven mostly by ~$72B+ in non-cash fair-value charges on convertible notes, derivatives and SAFEs — not cash burn.

Separately, on October 26, Bank of America, Blackstone, Citi and Morgan Stanley arranged $63B of financing via a special-purpose vehicle to buy Broadcom AI chips and networking gear for lease to Anthropic, with deliveries from 2027 — roughly $43.5B senior debt and $19.5B second-lien. Oracle becomes an AI chip buffet and brings ChatGPT/Codex into its apps

At Oracle AI World, Oracle and Google said Google TPUs — 8t for training, 8i for inference — will run on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure from calendar 2027: the first hyperscale cloud outside Google to offer TPUs, in a multi-year, multi-gigawatt partnership. 03 / RESEARCH

Two rival claims on the Exponential Time Hypothesis — and a 43-million-line formalisation

#2 · Research · OpenAI / AnthropicAnnounced Oct 26–27Unreviewed — no independent expert verdict yet

OpenAI and Anthropic both claim their models refuted the Exponential Time Hypothesis

Unreviewed: no peer review and no independent expert verdict yet. A working group led by Russell Impagliazzo, Ramamohan Paturi and Ryan Williams is examining OpenAI’s claim and says its involvement is not certification. Treat both as claims, not results.

OpenAI announced on October 26 that its models had refuted the Exponential Time Hypothesis; hours later on October 27, Anthropic posted its own Claude-credited paper and Lean 4 proof reaching the same claim. Both labs date the core result to October 16 and formalised it in Lean.

What it would — and wouldn’t — mean: the result is purely asymptotic. It only beats brute force at astronomically large formula sizes, changes nothing for real SAT solvers, and does not settle P vs NP.

OpenAI also claimed two further October 26 results — a proof of Sogge’s local smoothing conjecture (which would settle versions of the Kakeya and Stein problems) and a Yang–Mills existence proof whose materials have not yet been released. Both are also unconfirmed.
