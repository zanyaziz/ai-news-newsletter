Anthropic weighs a new model before its IPO

Jev trades generated language for calibrated decisions

03 / POLICY & SAFETY

California advances controls while Washington pauses

#1 · California · Gavin NewsomSeptember 18, 2026

Newsom orders a frontier-AI “kill switch” framework

Gov. Gavin Newsom signed an executive order accelerating two California AI safety laws, requiring an independent verifier inside frontier labs, third-party-written safety plans and an expert panel to recommend an AI safety guide within two months. The order also advances a formal emergency “kill switch” if models go rogue, making California’s approach consequential for OpenAI and Anthropic while Newsom presses Congress for national rules.

Key players: Gov. Gavin Newsom; California-based OpenAI and Anthropic.

Why it matters

The country’s largest tech hub is moving from broad safety principles toward operational controls and independent oversight.

#8 · 10th Circuit · Legal AISeptember 18, 2026

Federal appeals court proposes human-review certification for AI filings

The 10th U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals proposed requiring lawyers and self-represented litigants to certify that humans checked any generative-AI-assisted filing for accuracy. If adopted for January 1, 2027 across Colorado, Kansas, Oklahoma, New Mexico, Utah and Wyoming, it would be the first such rule among the 13 federal appeals courts — a direct institutional response to fabricated case citations.

Key players: The 10th Circuit and clerk Chris Wolpert.

04 / RESEARCH

Forecasting AI pulls ahead of people

#7 · Mantic · MetaculusSeptember 18, 2026

Mantic raises $25M after beating human forecasters

London-based Mantic beat every human entrant and all but one bot in the summer 2026 Metaculus Cup, the first tournament in which AI entrants dominated, then raised a $25 million seed round led by Radical Ventures. The result matters because Mantic specializes frontier models for calibrated forecasts and showed an edge over herd behavior, suggesting that prediction may become a distinct, commercially valuable AI capability.

Key players: Mantic and CEO Toby Shevlane; Radical Ventures, Microsoft M12, Thinking Machines Lab, Balderton Capital and Metaculus.

Why it matters

Forecasting systems could alter how governments and businesses plan — if strong tournament performance transfers to consequential real-world decisions.
