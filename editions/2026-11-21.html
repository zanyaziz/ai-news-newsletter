01 / FUNDING & BUSINESS

Record money, East and West

#1 · Business · AnthropicFri, Nov 20, 2026

Anthropic closes record IPO with $119.5B in gross proceeds

Anthropic announced the closing of its IPO on Friday, with underwriters fully exercising their greenshoe option for 147 million additional shares. The IPO alone raised about $93.5 billion — 1.127 billion Class A shares at $83 each — and a concurrent $26 billion private placement of Class N shares to AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC Partners brought total gross proceeds to roughly $119.5 billion, the largest IPO on record. The stock, trading on Nasdaq as ANT since November 19, closed Friday at $88.91, down 8.5% amid a broad tech sell-off, after closing its first session at $97.17. Anthropic now holds an estimated ~$250 billion in cash and short-term investments, most of it earmarked for compute.

Key people/companies: Anthropic; AMD; Broadcom; Google; Micron; Microsoft; Nvidia; TSMC Partners.

02 / MODEL RELEASES

Open weights flood in

#3 · Models · Thinking Machines LabFri, Nov 20, 2026Benchmarks self-reported

Thinking Machines Lab open-sources Inkling 2 Mini, a 26B looped model that runs on a laptop

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Mini, an open-weight multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 26.4B total parameters and only 3.4B active per token, whose middle 12 layers loop three times per token. The ~20GB checkpoint runs on a 32GB Apple Silicon Mac or a single Nvidia DGX Spark, supports up to 1M-token context, and is licensed Apache 2.0. In the company’s own benchmarks it leads all open models under 100B parameters on agentic coding tests — 48.2% on DeepSWE v1.1 and 79.2% on SWE-bench Verified.

Caveat: benchmark claims are self-reported by Thinking Machines Lab.

Key people/companies: Thinking Machines Lab.

#4 · Models · TencentFri, Nov 20, 2026Custom Tencent Hy License — not Apache 2.0

Tencent publishes Hy4’s full 770B-parameter weights

Tencent released the downloadable weights for Hy4, its flagship multimodal mixture-of-experts model — 770B total parameters, 49B active per token, ~1M-token context — including the instruct model, an FP8 build and a pre-trained base, on Hugging Face, ModelScope and GitCode. Independent testing found Hy4 ties Moonshot’s Kimi K3.1 Pro as the most accurate open model tested, while needing about a quarter of the memory.

Caveat: the license is the custom Tencent Hy License v1.0, not Apache 2.0 — a separate written licence is required for model-as-a-service operators above $50M revenue or 100M monthly users.

Key people/companies: Tencent.

03 / POLICY & SAFETY

Privacy alarms and disclosure bills

#5 · Safety · MetaThu, Nov 19, 2026Leak vs. hallucination unresolved; Meta disputes cross-user leak

Meta’s Muse agent accused of mixing up users’ private data, Reuters reports

Reuters reported, citing more than two dozen internal Meta posts, that employees using Meta’s personal AI agent Muse found it surfacing messages about childcare, career advice, product orders and bank balances they never requested — in one case inserting another person’s apparent Capital One ($2,722.58) and Charles Schwab ($5,899.57) balances into a chat. Other reports include a reminder to reply to a sister on Signal — an app the user said was never connected — and an unauthorized non-refundable Airbnb booking. Meta called the incidents “isolated” and said many flagged messages were hallucinations.

Caveat: Reuters could not establish whether the bank balances were leaked from another user or invented by the model; Meta disputes that any cross-user leak occurred.

Key people/companies: Meta (Muse); Reuters; Signal; AI Now Institute.

#10 · Policy · CongressNov 19–20, 2026Proposals / outline stage — not law

Senators introduce AUDIT AI Act on AI investment disclosure; House outlines SAFE AI Act

A bipartisan group of senators — Mark Warner, Angela Alsobrooks, John Kennedy and Mike Rounds — introduced the AUDIT AI Act, which would direct the SEC to write rules requiring companies to disclose AI infrastructure financing arrangements, AI governance policies and board oversight, plus a standardized AI risk section in Form 10-K filings. Separately, a bipartisan House trio (Reps. Jacobs, Maloy and Beyer) outlined the SAFE AI Act, which would require frontier labs to publish safety frameworks, pass government-overseen evaluations and report incidents.

Caveat: both measures are at the proposal/outline stage — neither imposes requirements unless enacted, and no SAFE AI bill text exists yet.

Key people/companies: Sens. Warner, Alsobrooks, Kennedy, Rounds; Reps. Jacobs, Maloy, Beyer; SEC.

04 / RESEARCH

Guarding reasoning, testing chemistry

#7 · Research · OpenAIFri, Nov 20, 2026OpenAI’s own internal evaluation

OpenAI details anti-distillation defences for GPT-6.2 Astra

OpenAI published “Combatting adversarial distillation,” describing defences for GPT-6.2 Astra that combine adversarial testing, model training, classifiers and API-level protections, including conversation-bound encrypted reasoning and streaming tool-call leak classifiers. In internal adaptive evaluation with strong automated red-teaming, the average success rate at recovering protected reasoning fell from 54.0% against GPT-6 Astra with its original safeguards to 0.2% against GPT-6.2 Astra with the updated ones.

Caveat: figures come from OpenAI’s own internal evaluation, not independent verification.

Key people/companies: OpenAI; GPT-6.2 Astra.

#11 · Research · OpenAIFri, Nov 20, 2026Single-sourced; scores are OpenAI’s own

OpenAI introduces MedChemBench for medicinal-chemistry reasoning

OpenAI introduced MedChemBench, a benchmark evaluating models on 99 literature-derived medicinal-chemistry problems covering 761 molecules. GPT-6.2 Astra led the 11 GPT models tested, scoring 84.5% on the “xhigh” credit metric — a concrete yardstick for pharma and biotech teams judging which models can actually do medicinal chemistry.

Caveat: single-sourced to the AI Compass digest; benchmark scores are OpenAI’s own.

Key people/companies: OpenAI.

05 / PRODUCTS & COMPANY

Agents move into the browser

#8 · Products · PerplexityFri, Nov 20, 2026

Perplexity brings its Computer Agent to Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc

Perplexity’s Computer AI agent — previously tied to its Comet browser and desktop apps — now works inside standard Chromium browsers via a Chrome Web Store extension, no Comet install needed. Users start tasks from a browser side panel and the agent drives tabs and logged-in sites directly. Perplexity also announced Brain (November 18), a self-improving system that writes and updates personal agent skills from past sessions.

Key people/companies: Perplexity; Aravind Srinivas.

Google opens Genie 4 to developers via the Gemini API

Google opened Genie 4, its interactive world model, to developers through the Gemini API (v1beta Interactions API) and Google AI Studio. Developers can build interactive 720p worlds with live audio, billed at roughly $0.10 per second including stereo audio, with sessions capped at 120 seconds. Google also launched Tool Retrieval in preview on November 20 across Gemini 3.6 and all Gemini 4 models. OpenAI meanwhile shipped GPT-Live-1.1, its updated full-duplex voice model for realtime agents.
