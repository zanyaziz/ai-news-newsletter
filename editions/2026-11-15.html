Lead · NvidiaThu, Nov 12, 2026Reason officially unstated — context is press reporting

Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO after 33 years

Nvidia's board announced founder-CEO Jensen Huang resigned as president, CEO and board member effective immediately after 33 years. CFO Colette Kress serves as interim CEO while remaining CFO; Harvey C. Jones becomes Nvidia's first-ever board chair. The official announcement gave no reason; it followed a Nov 8 board announcement of an independent investigation, and press reporting links the controversy to an alleged relationship with a Singapore-based tech executive U.S. intelligence officials suspect of spying for China. Shares fell more than 13%. Nvidia reports Q3 fiscal 2027 results Tuesday, Nov 17.

Why it matters: the most consequential leadership change in the AI industry in years, with questions over strategy, the earnings call, the pending Hugging Face acquisition, and Nvidia's role funding AI startups.

Caveat: the reason is officially unstated; the affair/espionage context is press reporting, not established fact.

Key people/companies: Nvidia, Jensen Huang, Colette Kress, Harvey C. Jones.

01 / AGENTS & COMMERCE

Agents get a wallet — and meet the banks

#1 · Agents · OpenAIFri, Nov 13, 2026

OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet

Dots agents can now complete purchases for US ChatGPT Pro subscribers: add a card once, each purchase needs user review plus a biometric check (Face ID / fingerprint), and networks issue a per-transaction token so neither agent nor merchant sees the real card number. Launch is US-only, gradual rollout, via Dots (also Work and Codex). Full delegation within preset spending limits is on the roadmap.

Why it matters: the first time a mainstream assistant can move real money with per-transaction approval — agents become economic actors.

Key people/companies: OpenAI, Dots.

#2 · Agents · JPMorganFri, Nov 13, 2026 · WSJ

JPMorgan blocks AI agents from using customers' credit cards

JPMorgan Chase, the largest US card issuer, has been blocking AI agents when consumers hand bots their card info; other large banks have also restricted agents' access to deposit accounts. Stripe routes around it with Link single-use virtual cards. The Treasury is asking banks about AI-payment frameworks and the OCC listed “autonomous payment agents” at its highest risk tier.

Why it matters: the same day OpenAI shipped its wallet, the biggest US bank said no — the agent-commerce era's first structural roadblock.

Key people/companies: JPMorgan Chase, Stripe, U.S. Treasury, OCC.

#7 · Research · Anthropic / GödelFri, Nov 13, 2026

Claude helps uncover 663 theorems in Gödel's notebooks

Helsinki logician Jan von Plato, transcribing 1,009 pages of Gödel's unpublished early-1940s notebooks (mostly in obsolete Gabelsberger shorthand) at about one page a week since 2017, fed transcriptions into Claude in September; within days Claude produced a 650-page digest and extracted 663 theorem statements. Checked in the Coq proof verifier, about 400 passed as written and ~200 after minor repairs, including a near-complete proof of the independence of the axiom of choice; ~50 were false.

Why it matters: eight years of work compressed into days — a clean, verifiable AI research-acceleration win.

Key people/companies: Jan von Plato, Kurt Gödel, Anthropic / Claude.

#15 · Safety · FBIFri, Nov 13, 2026

FBI issues first FLASH alert on AI-driven autonomous hacking — ARTEX

The FBI's first warning on AI-driven autonomous cyberattacks covers ARTEX, an open-source LLM-driven multi-agent penetration-testing framework weaponized to automate intrusions “at machine speed.” Since at least August 2026, varied actors used it across finance, government, tech, defense, semiconductors, energy, healthcare and more; some intrusions went from targeting to privileged access in under three hours. Over 1,600 publicly exposed ARTEX consoles were counted in early November.

Key people/companies: FBI, IC3, ARTEX.
