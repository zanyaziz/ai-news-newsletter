01 / POLICY, SAFETY & LEGAL

One campaign, 100+ breaches — and the governance scramble that followed

#1 · Safety · AI-agent hackingReuters · Oct 20What data was taken is not established

AI agents breach more than 100 company websites

Reuters, via NorForge research, reported Oct 20 that a Chinese-speaking hacker used the open-source Cairn platform — which coordinates AI agents, reported as a Claude Code harness driving DeepSeek V4.1 Flash — to breach more than 100 company websites after attempts on 1,200+. Reporters identified Deere, SharkNinja and Cummins among the targets.

The attack instructions were under 100 characters, and the whole campaign cost an estimated ~$5,000 in tokens — roughly $50 per breach. Caveat: what data was taken is not established. Deere said it saw no indication sensitive data was accessed, and SharkNinja said only a standalone support site was affected.

#2 · Safety · Microsoft agent governanceAnnounced Oct 20

Microsoft rolls out Guardian Agents to monitor and stop enterprise agents

Microsoft expanded Project Perception in Microsoft 365 E7 with Guardian Agents, Dynamic Permissions and Dynamic Agent Risk. Admins can now assess an agent’s risk, restrict it to read-only, require human approval, or shut an agent down — with detection covering 60 agent types, per GeekWire reporting.

Read against story #1, the pairing is the day in miniature: agents as the attack surface, and agents as the control layer watching them.

#4 · Deals · Salesforce / BrazeDefinitive agreement · Oct 20

Salesforce to acquire Braze for $5.66 billion

Salesforce announced a definitive agreement Oct 20 to acquire Braze for $47.50 cash per share — a ~58% premium and an enterprise value of about $5.66 billion. Braze stock jumped 51.7% to $45.57 on the news.

Braze serves roughly 2,770 customers, including Canva, Instacart and Venmo, and will be integrated with Marketing Cloud, Data 360 and Agentforce. The deal is expected to close in Salesforce’s fiscal 2028, subject to approvals.
