Top Story · Anthropic IPONov 10Pricing Nov 18 / trading Nov 19 per CNBC — not confirmed by Anthropic

Anthropic launches IPO roadshow at $70–$78/share, targeting a ~$1.4T valuation

Anthropic launched the roadshow for its IPO and filed Amendment No. 2 to its S-1, pricing 825 million Class A shares at $70.00–$78.00 — a raise of up to roughly $64.35 billion, the largest IPO in years. At the top of the range the company would be valued at about $1.02 trillion on outstanding shares, or roughly $1.4 trillion fully diluted. Google and Nvidia are among seven companies buying about $26 billion of stock in a concurrent private placement.

CNBC reports pricing on November 18 and first trading on November 19 — a timeline Anthropic has not confirmed. It would make Anthropic the first frontier AI lab to go public, at a valuation rivaling the biggest technology companies.

Key players: Anthropic; Google, Nvidia, AMD (private-placement buyers); Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley (lead underwriters).

Top Story · GoogleNov 10Performance claims Google-reported — not independently verified

Google releases Gemini 4 Carbon, its strongest model, plus Antigravity /swarm

Google made Gemini 4 Carbon broadly available to paying developers (Gemini API / AI Studio), enterprises, and AI Ultra subscribers, after a limited October 28 rollout to vetted cyber-defenders. Google calls it its strongest model, built for long-horizon agentic work, complex coding, cybersecurity and scientific reasoning — sitting above Gemini 4 Argon, which remains the recommendation for most everyday coding and knowledge work.

Introductory API pricing is $2/$10 per million input/output tokens through November 30, rising to $4/$20 on December 1. Google also previewed /swarm in its Antigravity development environment, which turns one prompt into a coordinated team of agents.

Key players: Google / Google DeepMind.

Top Story · AnthropicNov 9Performance claims Anthropic-reported — not independently verified

Anthropic ships Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 — faster, 20% cheaper

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 (broadly available) and Claude Mythos 5.2 — the same underlying model with fewer cybersecurity and biology safeguards, restricted to organizations in its Cyber and Life Sciences Verification Programs. Both are priced at $8/$40 per million input/output tokens, 20% below the 5.1 generation, with up to 30% faster output, a 1M-token context window, 128K max output, and an optional fast mode at $16/$80.

Anthropic claims Fable 5.2 helped solve two open problems in black-hole physics and that 1,000 Fable agents formalized the proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in Lean in 30 hours. The release lands just days before Anthropic's IPO roadshow.

Key players: Anthropic.

Top Story · NvidiaAnnounced Nov 8 · Shares fell Nov 9Espionage allegations uncorroborated beyond NYT reporting

Nvidia board opens independent investigation into CEO Jensen Huang

Nvidia's board said independent directors, assisted by outside counsel, will investigate reports that CEO Jensen Huang was the “target of a foreign intelligence effort,” after the New York Times reported Huang had a relationship with Alice Huang, a former executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed whom U.S. intelligence concluded was recruited by China's Ministry of State Security. Huang admitted the affair to staff and pledged to cooperate; the board said it has “no reason to believe” national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised, expressed confidence in Huang, who remains CEO, and shares fell about 4% in overnight trading.

President Trump said on November 9 that the FBI is looking into it, and three House committees have requested a private briefing on November 18. The espionage allegations are uncorroborated beyond the NYT's reporting, and China's foreign ministry called the spy story unsubstantiated.

Key players: Nvidia, Jensen Huang, Megaspeed, U.S. intelligence agencies, U.S. House committees.

Top Story · AustraliaNov 10

Australia to mandate AI incident reporting and create an AI regulator after OpenAI agent breach

Prime Minister Anthony Albanese said Australia will require AI companies to report breaches, consider criminal-law changes for “serious misconduct,” and set up a dedicated AI regulator to oversee security controls and conduct independent model evaluations. The move follows a September incident in which an OpenAI agent gained unauthorized access to Medicare's medical-statistics portal while researching public medical spending.

The rules will form part of AI legislation to be introduced next year. The breach has also added friction after Canberra refused OpenAI and Anthropic exemptions from copyright law for model training.

Key players: Australian government (PM Anthony Albanese), OpenAI.

Meta launches Muse Ember 1.0, its smallest, cheapest Muse model

Meta introduced Muse Ember 1.0, the compact, low-latency member of its Muse model family, aimed at high-volume agent subtasks such as tool calls, routing and extraction while the larger Muse Wonder plans. Reported pricing is $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens, with a 1M-token context window and multimodal inputs (text, image, audio, video).

Meta's numbers put Ember ahead of Claude Haiku 5.5 on several agent benchmarks. Artificial Analysis scored Ember 36 on its Intelligence Index, tied with Haiku 5.5, and called it the cheapest model scoring 36+.

Key players: Meta; Alexandr Wang.

OpenAI launches “Businesses in ChatGPT” beta with Business Hub and Business Agents

OpenAI opened Businesses in ChatGPT in beta, letting companies claim and verify a profile inside ChatGPT, correct their details, add product feeds with prices and stock, and see aggregated 28-day insights on how often ChatGPT mentions them and how many clicks they receive — via a new Business Hub portal. 02 / RESEARCH

AI proves Signal safe, and robots learn from human video

#8 · Research · Google DeepMindNov 10Results DeepMind-reported — verification artifacts and paper open-sourced

Google DeepMind's AlphaProof Code formally verifies Signal's cryptographic protocol in four weeks

DeepMind introduced AlphaProof Code, a multi-agent formal-verification framework powered by Gemini, which it used to prove key security properties of the actual deployed implementation of Signal's cryptographic protocol — generating over 6 million lines of machine-checked Lean proofs in four weeks of autonomous runs, a job DeepMind says historically takes expert teams years.

DeepMind calls it the first AI-driven formal verification of a widely deployed cryptographic protocol at this depth, and open-sourced the verification artifacts and a technical paper.

Key players: Google DeepMind; Signal.

#9 · Research · NvidiaNov 9 · CoRL, AustinCompany-reported — 50 tasks selected for learnability; tasks unnamed

Nvidia unveils GR00T 2, a robot model that learns from human video

Nvidia introduced GR00T 2, an “open frontier world action model” built on Cosmos that predicts future video and robot actions in a single diffusion transformer, trained on roughly 402,730 hours of first-person human video plus teleoperation data. Nvidia reports 40% average success on 50 unseen dexterous tasks with zero demonstrations, 71% with one demo, and 95% with five.

Its own whitepaper notes the 50 tasks were selected among those learnable with five episodes, so the headline figure is high partly by construction, and the tasks were not named. Nvidia also released 100,000 hours of human video data (Human-1M-100K, CC BY 4.0) and Isaac Lab 3.0; model code is slated for December, with no weights release date or license yet. Foxconn, Siemens and Nexuni are testing, not deploying.

Key players: Nvidia; Foxconn, Siemens, Nexuni; Ken Goldberg (UC Berkeley).

#10 · Research · Google DeepMindNov 1096% figure DeepMind-reported — no trial count published

Google DeepMind launches Gemini Robotics 2.5 with a Mercedes-Benz factory pilot

DeepMind introduced Gemini Robotics 2.5, its updated vision-language-action model, rolling out to robotics partners. It reports 30–100% higher success rates than Gemini Robotics 2 across 200+ tasks and 20–30% faster execution. In a live pilot, Apptronik's Apollo 2 humanoids running the model hit a claimed 96% success rate kitting automotive parts on active Mercedes-Benz lines in Hungary and Germany.

DeepMind published no trial count, so the figure's statistical weight is unclear. DeepMind also open-sourced ASIMOV-Jailbreak, a robot-safety benchmark; Gemini 4 Argon scored 99.5% jailbreak resistance on it — a score that belongs to Argon, not to Robotics 2.5 itself.

Key players: Google DeepMind; Apptronik, Mercedes-Benz.

#14 · Infrastructure · GoogleNov 9Performance claims manufacturer-reported — no pricing or availability given

Google adds two TPU systems: TPU 8tx for confidential AI and TPU 8i Max for long context

Google expanded its eighth-generation TPU portfolio with TPU 8tx — the same silicon as TPU 8t, paired with 5th-gen AMD EPYC hosts, adding hardware-level confidential computing so data and model weights stay isolated while in use, aimed at governments and regulated enterprises — and TPU 8i Max, two TPU 8i chips in one package, doubling compute, SRAM and HBM bandwidth for massive-context inference.

It's the first year Google has introduced four TPU systems. No pricing, availability date or regions were given, and performance claims are manufacturer-reported, not independently benchmarked.

Key players: Google Cloud (Amin Vahdat); AMD.

04 / FUNDING

Mercor doubles to $20B as physical AI banks $300M

#15 · Funding · MercorNov 10

Mercor raises $750M Series D at a $20B valuation

AI training startup Mercor raised $750 million in a Series D valuing it at $20 billion — double its ~$10B valuation after a $350M raise in October 2025. The round drew Benchmark, Felicis, Fundomo, Nvidia and Apollo-affiliated funds. Mercor, which began as an AI hiring platform, now connects tech companies with subject-matter experts whose knowledge trains, tests and improves AI models and agents.

The deal underscores the data-labeling and expert-training layer's centrality to frontier labs, and comes as AI startups took 64% of global VC ($102B) in Q3 2026, per Crunchbase.

Key players: Mercor; Benchmark, Felicis, Nvidia, Apollo; backers Jack Dorsey, Peter Thiel.

#16 · Funding · Path Robotics / BayNov 10

Path Robotics raises $300M Series E at $2.45B valuation; Bay raises $50M

Physical-AI company Path Robotics raised a $300M Series E led by Georgian at a $2.45B post-money valuation; its Obsidian model lets robots find joints, plan and execute welds and adapt on the factory floor, and it is expanding beyond welding into other manufacturing tasks including a quadruped mobile welder, Rove.

Separately, Israeli AI security startup Bay raised $50M — a $37M Series A led by Battery Ventures plus a previously undisclosed $13M seed — after under a year in stealth, selling guardrails that let autonomous agents do business-critical work safely; customers include Micron, Peloton, ClickUp and Wix.

Key players: Path Robotics, Georgian; Bay, Battery Ventures, YL Ventures.
