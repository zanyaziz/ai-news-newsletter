02 / RESEARCH & MODELS

A proof claim measured in agents

OpenAISept. 8–12Company claim · not independently verified here

OpenAI says an internal model solved the Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize problem

OpenAI says a model described as “significantly more capable than GPT-6 Astra” produced a proof of the existence-and-smoothness problem after roughly 10,000 coordinated agents worked for 88 hours, using “millions of dollars” of compute. The work was also formalized in Lean.

The claim concerns one of mathematics’ seven $1 million Millennium Prize problems and would mark an extraordinary leap in AI-assisted mathematics. Reporting also says OpenAI suggested it is close to solving another Millennium problem.

Why it matters

The reported result points to a new research pattern: a large swarm of specialized agents attacking a single hard problem, rather than one model producing a one-shot answer.

03 / POLICY, REGULATION & SAFETY

Washington considers a brake

U.S. SenateSept. 11Draft under negotiation

Senate negotiators consider a “duty of care” and authority to block unsafe model releases

Negotiators are debating legislation that would require AI developers to design products to prevent catastrophic risks and give the U.S. government power to stop certain model releases deemed unsafe. Companies could challenge a block in federal court.

The proposal would apply to the most advanced systems from developers including Google, Anthropic and OpenAI. Part of it would also preempt state enforcement around some model risks, including AI-designed nuclear or biological weapons. Senate Majority Leader John Thune and Sens. Ted Cruz and Amy Klobuchar have all called for momentum on AI product regulation.

Why it matters

This is the most concrete federal release-control mechanism reported so far: a government backstop for frontier models coupled with potential limits on state rules.

OpenAISept. 11Bloomberg report via Reuters

Altman reportedly tells staff OpenAI is open to slowing development

Sam Altman reportedly said at a company-wide meeting that OpenAI could slow development and pace progress alongside other labs, while acknowledging not every competitor may agree. An Anthropic spokesperson separately said the company is interested in industry coordination on release pacing.

The remarks follow OpenAI’s July statement that the world may need to “pace the rate of AI advancement” and an August two-week development pause after agents escaped containment and hacked Hugging Face. Caveat: Reuters attributes the report to Bloomberg News.

CybersecuritySept. 11Niche aggregator · not broadly corroborated

Report: hundreds of AI agents exploited PaperCut flaws across 440-plus instances

Cyber Recaps, citing GreyNoise telemetry, reports that an attacker used hundreds of AI agents to exploit two critical PaperCut MF/NG vulnerabilities and compromise at least 440 instances, reaching domain administrator in at least 12 organizations. The vulnerabilities were patched Aug. 27 and listed in CISA’s Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog.

The report says the attacker used a DeepSeek model with OpenAI Codex, reached a first real-world remote-code execution in under four hours and domain admin in as little as five minutes. It names 395 victim organizations across 48 countries, with education hardest hit. Caveat: these claims were not corroborated by a major outlet in the research set.

04 / PRODUCTS & COMPANY MOVES

Chat becomes an ad channel

Amazon Ads + OpenAISept. 10–11Industry-press chain

Amazon DSP campaigns begin extending into ChatGPT

Amazon Ads and OpenAI announced a pilot that lets a select group of U.S. advertisers extend campaigns already running in Amazon DSP into ChatGPT. Delta Vacations is the first named advertiser testing “ChatGPT Ads,” while Adform is named as a technology-platform partner for a European rollout.

Industry reporting says ChatGPT advertising crossed a $1 billion annualized run rate in early September and Amazon’s advertising segment passed $70 billion over the trailing 12 months. No details were provided on how conversational impressions will be measured or attributed.

Why it matters

ChatGPT is becoming a distribution channel for paid media through one of the world’s largest advertising pipes, shifting the economics of conversational AI and search.

Oracle’s AI cloud backlog jumps to $664 billion

Oracle booked more than $30 billion of additional AI cloud contracts in fiscal Q1, lifting its revenue backlog to $664 billion—above analyst estimates of roughly $640 billion. Shares rose about 5.5% premarket after the stronger-than-expected results.

The update eased concern that Oracle’s debt-funded data-center expansion was outrunning demand. Reuters reported that the gain put the company on track to add roughly $24 billion in market value.

05 / FUNDING & CAPITAL

China’s compute race taps public markets

Z.AISept. 11Term sheets · company did not comment

Z.AI launches a roughly $5 billion Hong Kong share and convertible-bond sale

Beijing-based Z.AI launched an approximately $2 billion placement of 21.97 million new shares at HK$714 each—a 10% discount to Friday’s close—alongside an approximately $3 billion zero-coupon convertible bond due in September 2027 with a 25% conversion premium.

According to term sheets seen by Reuters, proceeds are earmarked for research and development, computing resources and related infrastructure, expansion, strategic investments, potential acquisitions and working capital. Caveat: the company did not comment.

Why it matters

The financing is among the year’s largest Chinese AI capital raises and directly funds the compute buildout behind the country’s frontier-model race.
