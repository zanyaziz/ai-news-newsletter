02 / SAFETY, POLICY & POLITICS

Who decides when frontier AI is safe enough?

King Charles III · Nvidia · DeepMind · OpenAI · AnthropicSeptember 17, 2026

King Charles convenes AI leaders at Dumfries House

King Charles warned executives that AI carries “existential dangers” if it falls into the wrong hands and said the technology must remain “firmly in the service of humanity.” The Scottish summit brought together leaders expected to include Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis, OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar, representatives from Anthropic and the UK’s AI minister.

The king urged participants to put safeguards in place before it is “too late,” elevating the industry’s safety dispute from corporate statements to a high-profile international forum.

Why it matters

The meeting puts the same rival labs and chipmakers now disagreeing in public into one room to discuss shared guardrails.

Amazon told Reuters that AI systems should launch only when they are “ready and safe to use,” after rigorous testing and with strong safeguards. The company did not endorse Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei’s call to slow frontier development, separating support for safety engineering from support for collective pacing.
