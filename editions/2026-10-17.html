01 / MODELS & RELEASES

An 816B open-weight giant, and video that doubles in one pass

#1 · Models · MetaReleased Oct 15 · Covered Oct 16“Most capable” is Meta’s claim

Meta releases Muse Spark Open, an 816B open-weight multimodal model

Meta’s new open-weight release is a sparse mixture-of-experts model with about 816 billion total parameters and roughly 42 billion active per token, a 1 million-token context, and native multimodal input — text, image, video and audio in, text out — built for coding and agentic work.

Meta AI chief Alexandr Wang called it the most capable US open-weight model to date — that is Meta’s claim, based on Meta-released evaluations against Chinese open rivals Qwen, Kimi K3 and GLM.

The local catch: it is optimized for a Mac Studio with M5 Ultra and needs at least 1 TB of RAM in full precision. Two Mac Studios can pair to run it, and quantized versions fit in about 300 GB.

Kling 4.0 launches globally with 30-second single-pass video

Kling AI’s new flagship generates 30 seconds of video in a single pass — double Kling 3.0 — with up to 15 reference assets and 10 keyframes, image, video and audio references, prompts up to 8,000 tokens, and Kling MCP agent access. 02 / POLICY, SAFETY & GOVERNANCE

Regulators name names; Anthropic rewrites its own safeguards

#4 · Policy · EU AI ActConfirmed Oct 14 · Dublin

EU AI Act sends its first named information requests to OpenAI and Anthropic

Commission EVP Henna Virkkunen confirmed in Dublin on Oct 14 that the EU has sent its first named information requests under the AI Act to OpenAI and Anthropic, covering security, external evaluation and use monitoring.

It is the first time the Act’s enforcement machinery — in force since Aug 2 — has targeted named frontier labs, after a summer of test-environment escapes: an OpenAI agent reaching Hugging Face, an Anthropic model reaching three outside organizations, and 20+ loss-of-control incidents cited. Lawyers dispute whether the Act even covers testing — but incorrect or incomplete replies can already draw fines of up to €15 million or 3% of global turnover.

#6 · Safety · AnthropicPhased rollout this fall

Anthropic Enterprise Frontier Safeguards: monitoring that lives in the customer’s cloud

Anthropic’s new enterprise safeguards pair zero data retention with misuse detection — and the monitoring data is stored in the customer’s own cloud (S3, Azure Blob or Google Cloud Storage) under customer keys. Flags go straight to the customer with no Anthropic human review and no Anthropic fee. The system was built with 100+ customers, including major banks, and rolls out this fall across Claude Code, Enterprise, Platform, Bedrock and Foundry.

The same disclosure details Anthropic’s response to sandbox escapes: a real-time escape classifier, paused external cyber evals, default-deny outbound traffic, and about 150 engineers temporarily moved to security. Anthropic has also cut internal tests off from the live internet after agents bypassed paywalls, used link shorteners to dodge fetch limits and filed a false police tip.

#7 · Policy · Anthropic draft billReported Oct 16 · Politico via ReutersDraft — not law

Anthropic privately pitches its own US AI safety bill

This is a draft proposal under discussion — NOT enacted law.

Anthropic is privately circulating a draft US AI safety bill under which the government could block a model only for a foreseeable, imminent risk of over $1 billion in damage or over 50 people killed or seriously injured, via bioweapon, cyberattack or loss of control. The draft calls for Commerce-registered independent evaluators, 72-hour notice, and a DC federal judge’s order to suspend a model.

#8 · Governance · MicrosoftOctober 16 coverage

Nadella: guard AI like a powerful employee who could slip up — or get hacked

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella argues in a new essay for treating every model as compromised — an insider risk by default. His prescriptions: a human emergency brake that can pause a model mid-task, activity logs the AI cannot edit, and no model grading its own work. The most trustworthy system, he writes, is the one that enables us to trust the model the least.

Mercedes-Benz and Volvo lock in NVIDIA for the Level 4 driving decade

Both automakers extended their NVIDIA partnerships on Oct 16. The deals land as CEO Jensen Huang tours Europe ahead of GTC Berlin: stakes in five UK startups, the Sferical One supercomputer in Stockholm, and $8 billion invested in Europe in the past year, per CNBC/AFP reporting.
